Assumption ledger

Paint the states you are assuming, mark the capitols you are assuming — including the hidden ones — and every published number is re-checked against the real travel costs. The capitol map shows where a capitol could be; this page shows what your assumptions cost you.

Original · 11×11no states yet

Top-left of a square — the area of the state it is in; a ✱ means that state has no capitol (its symmetries fix more than one square, or none), so it contributes no destination and every step inside it costs the whole area. Bottom-right — the cheapest distance from that square to a capitol we already know about, if every unknown square were as small as possible. It is a floor, not the answer: a hidden capitol can be nearer, which is exactly what a number below that floor demands.
